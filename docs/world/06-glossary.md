# 06 — Glossary (cap: 25 entries)

Stressed syllables are in CAPS. Check here before adding any name, and never borrow one from a published setting (`00-tone.md`, don't #5). Visual descriptions live in each term's source file.

1. **Tethrane** (TETH-rain): the Empire, and its adjective.
2. **the Throne**: the distant imperial government, always unnamed.
3. **Frontier Line**: the Empire's March garrison. Soldiers say "the Line".
4. **the Notch**: the Line's insignia, a teal square with one corner cut away.
5. **Vellan March** (VEL-un): the MVP region.
6. **Oskett** (OSS-kit): the Hessane village of Level 1.
7. **Fort Dremmel** (DREM-ul): the half-empty garrison above Oskett.
8. **Eastern Road**: the imperial road cut through the barrows.
9. **Hollow Choir**: the singing barrow complex (Level 2).
10. **Longsteps**: the chest-high black terraces beyond the Choir.
11. **Hessane** (HESS-ahn): the March's people and their language.
12. **Keeper**: a Hessane barrow rite-keeper.
13. **Idrenna** (ih-DREN-uh): Keeper of Oskett, the "Elder" NPC.
14. **ward-stone**: a standing stone that, once lit, keeps the dead down.
15. **Rite Ban**: the 38-year-old imperial ban on Hessane rites.
16. **Lantern Charter**: antiquarian delvers holding an imperial writ.
17. **Reach** (REECH): a magical realm; "to reach" is to use one.
18. **Lowfire** (LOH-fire): the Reach of banked heat; the player's only Reach.
19. **Kindle**: the player's Lowfire ability, on a cooldown.
20. **Stillreach** (STILL-reech): the Reach of stopped time that raises the dead.
21. **Still-pool**: a ground patch where fallen levies re-form.
22. **the Sleet**: the Reach of cold water, used by mages and Charter lamps.
23. **Understair** (UN-der-stair): the unheld Reach of the Longsteps.
24. **Barrow-levy** (BARR-oh LEV-ee): a risen dead soldier of the terraces; Front-file (melee) or Back-file (archer).
25. **Sett-boar** (SET-bor): the March's burrowing quadruped.
