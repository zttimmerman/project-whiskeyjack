# 04 — History (the last ~50 years)

Years before the game starts. The Line keeps the dates; the Hessane keep the grudges.

- **52 — the Rain Campaign.** The Empire annexes the March. Fort Dremmel's founding stone reads *"To secure the Eastern Road."* Nobody alive knows what lay east worth securing.
- **47 — the Road is cut** straight through the terraces, breaching about a dozen barrows. Reports of "walkers" are logged as desertion.
- **38 — the Rite Ban.** After a tithe riot at Oskett, Hessane gatherings are outlawed. The ward-stones go cold.
- **25 — the Southern Draw.** Most of the garrison marches to a war on the salt coast, which is still going. The March becomes a posting for the old, the punished and the unlucky.
- **11 — the Charter's writ.** The Lantern Charter arrives with authority to excavate; the Line is ordered to assist.
- **6 — the Wet Winter.** The Charter opens the Hollow Choir. Risings become regular.
- **2 — the last dispatch.** Pay for half the garrison, and no new orders.
- **Now.** Fort Dremmel is at a third of its strength. The player's squad has just arrived; the first duty is road patrol to Oskett.

**Moral shape:** the Empire caused the risings twice, with the road and the ban, and its soldiers didn't do either but die for both. The rites worked, and the Keepers also ran a tithe revolt. The Charter may be the Empire's only hope, and it's making everything worse.

## Deep-time mysteries (never resolved in the MVP)

Characters may guess; text never confirms. At most one wrong guess per area.

### The chest-high stair
The Longsteps' risers are too tall for human legs, and the black stone never weathers. The Hessane say the terraces were "cut for walking, not farming", and won't say by whom.

A lone soldier stands dwarfed at a Peat Black terrace wall taller than their helm, a hand flat on stone that stays dry in the rain. The seams run Tarnished Gold, and fog pours off the step above like water over a weir.

### The borrowed rites
The rite-words are in a language no living Hessane speaks, always sung facing east. Keepers say the rites were *given*.

A hooded Keeper kneels facing east at a cold ward-stone, three soot lines on her brow. The stone's worn carvings are older and sharper than any Hessane charm. Grey dawn, Peat Black ground, one Torch Amber candle stub.

### The Choir is counting
The Hollow Choir's wind-song repeats a sequence of notes, and old Keepers swear it gets one note shorter each year. The Charter has started writing it down.

A grey shaft of light falls onto rows of Old Bone niches in a Rain Stone vault. A Charter surveyor raises a Tarnished Gold lamp to a wall of chalk tallies, many crossed out. Dust hangs in the light.
