# 01 — Geography: the Vellan March

A cold, rain-soaked frontier upland on the Tethrane Empire's eastern edge.

**Climate:** Maritime and wet. It drizzles most days, mornings are foggy, and sleet falls on the high terraces in winter, with little snow. The ground is peat over stone, with bogs in every undrained hollow.

**Landform:** Hills cut into long **terraces**, far older than anyone living. **Barrows** (turf mounds with stone doors) line almost every terrace lip, in rows too regular for a village graveyard.

**Visual rule:** horizontal lines everywhere (terraces, roofs, walls), broken by vertical accents: barrow doors, milestones, banner poles. Cool muted colors dominate; Torch Amber means people.

---

## Oskett *(Level 1 start)*

A Hessane barrow-keeping village of about forty people, built into one terrace step. Soot-marked **ward-stones** at the edges have been cold since the Rite Ban.
*Gameplay:* safe hub, the Keeper's quest, tutorial space.

Squat Rain Stone longhouses dug into a terrace wall, their turf roofs green-black with rain under Peat Black smoke. Barrow Oak racks hold Saddle Leather hides, and waist-high standing stones carry old soot rings. Torch Amber doorways glow against a wet grey dusk.

## The Eastern Road *(Level 1 corridors and exit)*

The imperial road runs straight east and ignores the terraces. Wherever its builders met a barrow, they cut through it, and this is where the dead walk.
*Gameplay:* combat corridor, the quest's reach trigger, the sealed exit toward the Choir.

Cracked, sunken Wet Slate blocks laid over older curved paving, in a trench through the terraces. Split turf mounds line both sides, Old Bone showing in the spill. Milestones flake Garrison Teal paint, and puddles reflect an Overcast Blue sky.

## The Hollow Choir *(Level 2 crypt)*

The March's largest barrow complex. Wind shafts make it sing in weather, and the dead lie in niches rank upon rank. The Lantern Charter opened it six years ago.
*Gameplay:* dungeon, archer-heavy halls, future Choir-warden elite.

Long Rain Stone vaults lined with niches, each holding a pale Old Bone shape. Tarnished Gold candelabra gutter Torch Amber, and narrow shafts drop grey light. Soot, dust and Charter chalk marks cover everything.

## Fort Dremmel *(post-MVP)*

The imperial garrison above Oskett, built for three hundred and holding about a hundred. It's the player's barracks.
*Gameplay:* future hub, with quartermaster, squad and job board.

A square palisade of blackened Barrow Oak on a Wet Slate footing, with one stone tower. A long banner once Garrison Teal hangs soaked and faded nearly grey. Muddy yards, patched canvas, one lit window.

## The Longsteps *(post-MVP; deep time)*

Beyond the Choir, the terraces turn to black, flat-faced stone, each step chest-high. No barrows, no people, no birds.
*Gameplay:* future late area with Understair puzzles.

Enormous Peat Black terraces with unnaturally flat faces, every riser taller than a person. Hairline seams of Tarnished Gold run untouched by rust or moss. Fog pools on each step, dwarfing any figure.
