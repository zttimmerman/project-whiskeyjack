# 00 — Tone

**Register: wet boots, dry jokes.** The war is old, the rain never stops, and nobody here is important. We stay at a soldier's eye level: whose watch it is, what's in the pot, who didn't come back. Grief is carried, not performed. Humor is how people admit fear. Nobody is evil for pleasure; everyone is doing the job they were left holding.

**Darkness is implied.** Show aftermath, not the act: an empty boot by a milestone, a tally scratched into a bunk. At most once per area, someone says one blunt, true thing, and the scene moves on.

## Do

1. **Write from the bottom of the chain.** Rations, feet, weather, orders. Big events arrive as rumor, usually wrong.
2. **Show compassion as a verb.** Dry socks, a shared pipe, a watch taken that wasn't yours.
3. **Flatten the joke as things get worse.** Understated, never mugging.
4. **Give every speaker a reason.** Keeper, soldier and delver each think they're holding things together, and each is partly right.
5. **Keep it short.** At most 2 sentences per dialogue node; objectives imperative and 12 words or fewer.

## Don't

1. **No chosen ones.** No prophecy, no "only you can…". The player is a soldier with a company number.
2. **No villain monologues** and no clean good/evil. Enemies can be right.
3. **No lore dumps.** Never explain the deep past; people guess wrong.
4. **No modern slang, meta winks, or lingering gore.**
5. **No borrowed names** from *Malazan Book of the Fallen* or any published setting: no names, terms, gods, factions or events. Never use the project codename in-world. Check `06-glossary.md` first.

## Voice samples

- *Quartermaster:* "Boots are dry. Don't ask how. Don't ask the lad who had them."
- *Keeper:* "We sang them down for three hundred years. Your people stopped us in a day."
- *Corporal:* "Orders say hold the road. Doesn't say from what, anymore."

**Visual mood:** Low grey cloud over stepped hills of Rain Stone and Peat Black, everything dark with wet. Small points of Torch Amber mark people, with faded Garrison Teal cloth hung to dry. Nothing is new; every edge is worn round.
