# 02 — Factions

Each faction needs the others, and each makes the others' problems worse.

| | Barrows should be… | The Line should be… |
|---|---|---|
| **Frontier Line** | quiet, cheaply | paid and sent home |
| **Hessane** | sung shut | gone, but not yet |
| **Lantern Charter** | opened | out of the way |

---

## The Tethrane Frontier Line *(the player's side)*

The Empire's garrison at Fort Dremmel. It was sent fifty years ago to "secure the Eastern Road", and nobody remembers from what. The capital last wrote two years ago; the tithe now mostly pays the garrison itself.
**Aims:** hold the road cheaply and survive until relieved.
**Conflicts:** it enforces the Rite Ban against the Hessane, and it's legally subordinate to the Charter while bleeding for the risings the Charter causes.
**Gameplay hook:** quest givers and rewards, with future squad allies. Line **Deserters** are a human enemy that uses the player's own moveset.
**Visual signature:** half-helm with a neck flap, knee-length tunic, rectangular shield · Garrison Teal, Saddle Leather, Blackened Iron · wool, oiled leather, riveted iron · insignia **the Notch**, a teal square with one corner cut away and the company number stitched below.

Soldiers in rain-darkened Garrison Teal wool tunics over Saddle Leather harness and Blackened Iron half-helms. Barrow Oak shields carry a notched teal square, chipped and repainted many times. Mud to the knee, patched elbows, everything a size too big.

## The Hessane *(the March's people)*

Terrace farmers and hereditary barrow-keepers. Their **Keepers'** rites and lit **ward-stones** kept the dead down until the Empire banned the rites thirty-eight years ago. A few Keepers still practice them in secret.
**Aims:** keep the dead asleep, restore the rites, and see the Empire leave, but only once the dead are quiet.
**Conflicts:** they defy the Line's ban and sabotage the Charter's digs.
**Gameplay hook:** Keepers give quests. A **ward-stone**, once lit with the player's Kindle, stops fallen Barrow-levies within its radius from rising again. Relighting stones is how cleared ground stays cleared.
**Visual signature:** tall peaked hood, a fringe of bone charms, a staff · Peat Black, Old Bone, Barrow Oak · felted wool, oilskin, carved bone · insignia: three stacked horizontal soot lines (the terraces) on doors, stones and brows.

Figures in heavy Peat Black oilskin cloaks with tall peaked hoods and mud-heavy hems. Small Old Bone charms clack along their shoulders, and their Barrow Oak staves are worn pale at the grip. Three soot lines are smudged across the brow.

## The Lantern Charter *(antiquarian delvers)*

Scholars and hired diggers with an imperial writ to open the barrows and recover relics of the terrace-builders, which the capital hopes can save a failing Empire.
**Aims:** open the Hollow Choir and reach the Longsteps.
**Conflicts:** the Hessane call the digging sacrilege, correctly, since it wakes the dead. The Line must protect the Charter by law and clean up after it in practice.
**Gameplay hook:** dig camps are hostile or neutral encounters. **Sleet-lamps** create fog that shortens detection and lock-on range for everyone; breaking a lamp clears it. Later, the Charter buys relics.
**Visual signature:** wide hat, long coat, pack frame with a lamp on a pole overhead (tall and lopsided at any distance) · Rain Stone, Tarnished Gold, Overcast Blue · waxed canvas, brass, glass · insignia: a brass lamp stamped with a key.

Delvers in long Rain Stone canvas coats and wide dripping hats, bent under pack frames. Each carries a Tarnished Gold lamp raised on a pole, glowing cold Overcast Blue. Chalk-dusted gloves, and brass tags wired to every tool.
